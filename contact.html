Before you write

Do you accept unsolicited story pitches? +
Yes — we welcome pitches from writers, architects, and designers. Please include a short summary (200 words max), your intended angle, and a brief bio. We respond to all pitches within two weeks.
How can I advertise or partner with Foundations of Design? +
We offer limited, carefully curated partnerships with brands aligned with our editorial values. Please write to press@foundationsofdesign.com with a short description of your brand and what you have in mind.
Can I republish your articles? +
We occasionally allow syndication. Please get in touch with details of your publication and the specific article you're interested in. We do not allow full republishing without written permission.
Do you offer design consultations? +
Elena and the team occasionally take on select consulting projects. These are limited and typically long-form. If you'd like to explore working together, please describe your project in the form above and select "Collaboration".
