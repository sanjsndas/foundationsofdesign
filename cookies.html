Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Foundations of Design uses cookies and similar tracking technologies, and how you can manage them.

Last updated: 1 March 2025 Effective: 1 March 2025

Foundations of Design (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website www.foundationsofdesign.com. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding their use. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which provides more detail on how we handle your personal data.

By continuing to browse our website, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, you should disable cookies in your browser settings or refrain from using our site.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to improve user experience, and to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be “persistent” (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or “session” cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be “first-party” (set by us) or “third-party” (set by a domain other than ours).

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for several reasons, including:

  • Essential operation: To ensure our website functions correctly, such as remembering your cookie consent preferences and enabling basic navigation.
  • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with our site — which pages are most popular, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve our content and user experience.
  • Functionality: To remember your preferences (such as language or region) and provide enhanced, more personalised features.
  • Marketing and advertising: If applicable, to deliver relevant editorial promotions or to measure the effectiveness of our newsletter and social media campaigns. We do not use cookies for third-party behavioural advertising without your explicit consent.

3. Types of cookies we use

Below is a summary of the cookies we may set and their purposes. We keep our cookie usage minimal and privacy-conscious, in line with our editorial values.

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie preferences so we don't ask again on every visit. 1 year
session_id Essential Maintains your session while browsing, e.g., for contact form security. Session
_ga / _gid Analytics Google Analytics — helps us understand visitor numbers and behaviour anonymously. 2 years / 24h
_fbp Marketing Used by Meta (Facebook) to measure the effectiveness of our social media presence. Only set if you interact with embedded content. 3 months
pref_theme Functional Remembers your display preferences (e.g., dark/light mode) if applicable. 6 months

Note: Third-party cookies (such as those from Google Analytics or social media platforms) are governed by the respective third party's privacy and cookie policies. We do not control these cookies.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: For anonymous traffic analysis. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.
  • Embedded content: Articles may include embedded content (e.g., Instagram posts, Pinterest pins, YouTube videos). These platforms may set their own cookies when you interact with the embedded content.
  • Newsletter platforms: If you subscribe, our email service provider may use tracking pixels or cookies to measure open rates, but only in aggregated form.

5. Managing and disabling cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though some functionality and areas may be restricted.

Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

To opt out of Google Analytics across all websites, visit https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Your consent: When you first visit our site, you may see a cookie banner allowing you to accept or manage your preferences. You can change your choices at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site, or by using the browser controls above.

6. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers support a “Do Not Track” (DNT) feature. Because there is no industry consensus on how to interpret DNT signals, our website does not currently respond to DNT browser settings. We do not track our visitors across third-party websites for advertising purposes.

7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
