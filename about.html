About the Journal

Spaces that hold meaning.
Ideas that build slowly.

Foundations of Design is an independent publication about interior architecture, materials, light, and the quiet decisions that shape how we live.

Our Mission

We believe that good design is not about trends or spectacle — it’s about the thoughtful arrangement of space, light, and material to support human life. Foundations of Design exists to slow down the conversation. We publish long-form essays, practical guides, and case studies that explore the fundamental principles of interior architecture: proportion, circulation, natural light, material honesty, and the psychology of colour and texture.

Our readers are design professionals, students, and curious enthusiasts who want more than surface-level inspiration. They come to us for depth, clarity, and a point of view that values longevity over novelty.

“We are interested in the quiet, structural decisions — the ones that don’t photograph well but make a room feel right.”

What We Cover

Our editorial scope is deliberately focused. We write about:

  • Interior design & architecture — residential and adaptive reuse, with an emphasis on spatial thinking.
  • Materials & craft — how natural and honest surfaces age, and why patina matters.
  • Lighting design — the layered approach, from daylight to artificial ambience.
  • Space planning — circulation, small spaces, and the unseen architecture of daily life.
  • Sustainable & passive design — low-tech, high-comfort strategies for real homes.
  • Furniture & layout — practical rules and when to break them.

How We Work

Foundations of Design is reader-supported and independent. We do not accept sponsored content that compromises our editorial integrity. Our articles are written by practicing architects, interior designers, and design journalists who bring both technical knowledge and a love of the craft.

We publish on a monthly cadence, with occasional long-form guides and interviews. Every piece is reviewed by our editorial team for accuracy, clarity, and usefulness. We believe in showing our work — footnotes, references, and real-world examples are part of our standard.

If you have a project, a material, or an idea you’d like to share, we’d love to hear from you. Visit our contact page or write to us directly.
